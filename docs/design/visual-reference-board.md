# V0 Visual Reference Board

Status: V0 working reference set

## How to use this board

This is an annotated direction board, not a list of assets already approved for the game. Each reference identifies one property to study and an explicit usage boundary. Do not feed a copyrighted reference image into a generation or conversion tool unless its license and the tool's terms permit that use.

License labels mean:

- **Observation only:** study the named principle; do not copy, trace, download into the repository, or use the image as a generation input.
- **Candidate source:** the page states a reusable license, but issue #8 must still retain the exact file, author, license snapshot, download date, and any required attribution before importing it.
- **Public domain reference:** the source marks the work public domain; retain source/object metadata if adapting it.

All links and stated page licenses were checked on 2026-07-20. A later download must be checked again.

## Annotated references

### R01 — Stylized character construction

- **Source:** Julien Kaspar, [Stylized Character Workflow — final render and turnarounds](https://studio.blender.org/training/stylized-character-workflow/5d28b0a4e774c2173c3ccecb/), Blender Studio.
- **Provenance:** official Blender Studio training page; author is named on the page.
- **Rights/usage:** **Observation only** for this issue. The page offers some free training/downloads, but any production-file license must be verified at the individual download before reuse.
- **Study:** large planar head forms, restrained surface detail, expression-friendly topology, and the value of clay/wire/turnaround checks.
- **Do not copy:** the character's identity, clothing, hair, proportions, palette, or topology file.
- **Apply to The Fall:** request clay and top-camera turnarounds before approving color polish; judge the shoulder/head/hand silhouette first.

### R02 — Table proportions and construction

- **Source:** Ethan Place, [Wooden Table 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/WoodenTable_01), Poly Haven.
- **Provenance:** asset page names the author and describes a 1.8 m table with 952 triangles.
- **Rights/usage:** **Candidate source — CC0** as stated on the asset page. Preserve author/source metadata even though attribution is not required.
- **Study:** economical support geometry, rounded worn edges, plausible stretchers, and how a simple prop reads with few triangles.
- **Do not copy:** the rectangular coffee-table proportions as the gameplay solution.
- **Apply to The Fall:** translate the economical construction into a round, seated card-table silhouette with an uncluttered centre.

### R03 — Wood scale and roughness

- **Source:** Dimitrios Savva, [Wood Table material](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table), Poly Haven.
- **Provenance:** asset page names the author, physical width, resolution, and available PBR maps.
- **Rights/usage:** **Candidate source — CC0** as stated on the asset page.
- **Study:** warm pine value range, knot frequency, rough-sawn marks, and coating response.
- **Do not copy:** high-frequency knots or displacement that compete with card faces.
- **Apply to The Fall:** simplify to fewer, broader grain bands; keep the central play field quieter than the rim.

### R04 — Warm room lighting

- **Source:** Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, [Warm Bar HDRI](https://polyhaven.com/a/warm_bar), Poly Haven.
- **Provenance:** asset page names photography/processing authors and records color temperature and exposure range.
- **Rights/usage:** **Candidate source — CC0** as stated on the asset page.
- **Study:** warm overhead ambience, medium contrast, soft wood highlights, and cooler dark separation.
- **Do not copy:** the modern bar fixtures, bottles, or architecture.
- **Apply to The Fall:** use the light relationship as a neutral look-development reference; build a fictional medieval-cartoon room with project-owned props.

### R05 — Spanish deck structure

- **Source:** [Spanish playing-card range](https://www.nhfournier.es/productos/naipe-espanol/), Naipes Heraclio Fournier.
- **Provenance:** official manufacturer page describing Spanish-deck variants and formats.
- **Rights/usage:** **Observation only.** Fournier's catalogue states that its Spanish-deck images are registered marks; no reuse license is granted by the product page.
- **Study:** immediate separation of Coins, Cups, Swords, and Clubs; large central suit objects; strong border rhythm; compact card proportions.
- **Do not copy:** illustrations, linework, borders, palette, packaging, logos, or exact suit artwork.
- **Apply to The Fall:** retain the rule-owned suit concepts while drawing original project-owned symbols and a new border system.

### R06 — Hand-painted card material

- **Source:** [The Cloisters Playing Cards](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/475513), The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 1983.515.1–.52.
- **Provenance:** museum collection record identifies South Netherlandish origin, materials, period, and object number.
- **Rights/usage:** **Public domain reference** as marked on the collection page.
- **Study:** opaque flat paint, limited palette, handmade line variation, and readable central motifs.
- **Do not copy:** its non-Spanish suit system or period figures into The Fall's deck.
- **Apply to The Fall:** evoke printed/painted tactility with clean digital shapes and limited texture, preserving mobile legibility.

### R07 — Sword construction

- **Source:** [Hand-and-a-Half Sword](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/35888), The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 14.25.1196.
- **Provenance:** museum record identifies materials, dimensions, and European or possibly British origin.
- **Rights/usage:** **Public domain reference** as marked on the collection page.
- **Study:** straight silhouette, clear guard/grip/pommel grouping, restrained metal/leather separation.
- **Do not copy:** identifying inscriptions or a complete historical reconstruction.
- **Apply to The Fall:** reduce the suit icon to three or four bold masses that survive at card-corner size.

### R08 — Cup construction

- **Source:** [Bowl of a Drinking Cup](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/471268), The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 47.101.31.
- **Provenance:** museum record identifies material, dimensions, and British or Scandinavian attribution.
- **Rights/usage:** **Public domain reference** as marked on the collection page.
- **Study:** wide bowl, narrow stem, foot, and simple reflective hierarchy.
- **Do not copy:** niello pattern or provenance-specific decoration.
- **Apply to The Fall:** exaggerate bowl/stem/foot so the Cup icon remains distinct from Coins at small sizes.

### R09 — Club construction

- **Source:** [Mace or Club (Mazzacorto)](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/26832), The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 04.44.
- **Provenance:** museum record identifies a painted wood object from Pisa.
- **Rights/usage:** **Public domain reference** as marked on the collection page.
- **Study:** weighted end, handle taper, and chunky silhouette.
- **Do not copy:** painted decoration, heraldic details, or event-specific history.
- **Apply to The Fall:** build a fictional wooden baton/club mark with a blunt, unmistakable top-heavy read.

### R10 — Reusable fantasy UI framing

- **Source:** [Fantasy UI Borders](https://kenney-assets.itch.io/fantasy-ui-borders), Kenney.
- **Provenance:** official author storefront; page states the pack contents and that no generative AI was used.
- **Rights/usage:** **Candidate source — CC0 1.0** as stated on the page.
- **Study:** nine-slice construction, shallow borders, modular corners, and separation between frame and content.
- **Do not copy:** a generic dark-fantasy palette wholesale or treat the pack as final The Fall UI.
- **Apply to The Fall:** use as a layout/prototyping candidate, then recolor or redraw to the project palette while protecting content area and localization expansion.

### R11 — VFX as information

- **Source:** Hadidjah Chamberlin, [Developer Insights: The Art Behind THE SCIENCE](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/22552047/developer-insights-the-art-behind-the-science), Blizzard Entertainment.
- **Provenance:** official developer article by a named senior effects artist.
- **Rights/usage:** **Observation only.** No asset-reuse license is granted.
- **Study:** effects communicate board state, persistent status, and celebratory peaks; effect families maintain a coherent identity.
- **Do not copy:** effect shapes, timing, colors, particles, card art, or branded presentation.
- **Apply to The Fall:** design capture trails that count cascade steps and reserve the strongest non-camera beat for Fall/clean-table results.

## Reference rules for generation prompts

- Prompts may name project-owned qualities such as "broad planar forms," "warm handcrafted wood," and the palette values in the art-direction brief.
- Prompts must not name a living artist or ask for an imitation of a commercial game's style.
- Observation-only images are never image inputs.
- A public-domain or CC0 file may become an input only after issue #8 creates its asset record and verifies the current file-level terms.
- Generated output receives its own provenance record; the references do not become the output's license.
