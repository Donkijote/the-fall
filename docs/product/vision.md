# Product Vision

Status: Draft

## Identity

**Confirmed:** The public title is **The Fall**.

The Fall is a from-scratch 3D rework of an earlier TypeScript/PixiJS interpretation of the traditional Venezuelan card game Caída. The old project is historical reference material only. Its implementation, architecture, and assets are not migration sources.

## Product promise

The Fall should turn a traditional table card game into a readable, expressive 3D experience where characters, cards, camera framing, animation, sound, and effects make every deal, capture, cascade, and Fall satisfying.

## Guiding principles

- **Rules before spectacle:** presentation must communicate the deterministic game outcome rather than decide it.
- **Readable 3D:** cinematic presentation must not hide cards, turns, scores, or available actions.
- **Deliberate animation:** important game events should feel distinct and sequenced, not like generic object movement.
- **Platform-aware interaction:** touch and desktop input should express the same intent through appropriate controls.
- **Culturally neutral world:** the visual setting is not tied to a specific real-world culture.
- **English source documentation:** project documentation is maintained in English.
- **Localization-ready UI:** player-facing text should be designed for future translation into multiple languages.

## Audience and modes

**Confirmed:** The long-term product includes:

- an offline Story Mode played against bots
- online player-versus-player multiplayer at a later stage
- 1v1, three-player free-for-all, and 2v2 game rules

Story Mode uses a predefined cast, progressively harder matches, and an offline tournament structure. Individual matches may introduce custom objectives or rule variations. Character creation and customization remain possible future-version features.

## Target platforms

**Confirmed:** Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS are intended platforms. See [platforms](../technical/platforms.md) for input and orientation requirements.

## Open product decisions

- age range and audience positioning
- tone and structure of Story Mode
- progression, rewards, and player profile scope
- whether local pass-and-play is supported
- business model and distribution strategy
- accessibility and localization launch requirements
