# UI icon attribution

The PNG icons in this directory are used under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
They were downloaded in the white-on-transparent variant from
[Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net/) and are tinted at runtime with The Fall's UI palette.

Icons by:

- Lorc: `canto`, `cups`, `envelope`, `gems`, `padlock`, `quest`, `rank`, `replay`, `settings`, and `shield`
- Delapouite: `audio`, `bag`, `clubs`, `coins`, `energy`, `enter-arrow`, `home`, `send`, `shop`, and `skip`
- Faithtoken: `decks`

No geometry or source artwork was modified. File names were normalized for their role in The Fall.
Individual source pages and the complete intake record are listed in
`docs/assets/ui-icon-library.md`.

The disconnected first-playable authentication marks are monochrome exports from
[Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), released under CC0:

- `google-g.png`: Simple Icons Google mark
- `apple-sign-in.png`: Simple Icons Apple mark

The names and marks remain trademarks of their respective owners. Replace these prototype assets
with provider-approved sign-in components or artwork before enabling production authentication.
